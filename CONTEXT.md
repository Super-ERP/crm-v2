# CRM sales language

The same sales records appear across Accounts, Opportunities, Funnels, Quotations and Projects. These terms name the business concepts consistently across screens and documentation.

## People and ownership

**Account owner**:
The organization member responsible for an account and its active related sales records. Ownership transfers to a new member when the account owner changes.
_Avoid_: Account manager, funnel owner, opportunity owner when referring to the inherited sales owner

**Salesperson**:
A person doing sales work; a salesperson may own an account, but the terms are not interchangeable.

**Reporting manager**:
A member in a salesperson's reporting line who may be selected to decide an approval when they have the required permission.
_Avoid_: Account manager

**Organization Owner**:
The system role with broad organization permissions. It does not imply ownership of every account.
_Avoid_: Account owner when referring to the system role

**Opportunity Owner Contact**:
A customer-side contact recorded as the opportunity's budget or decision owner. This person is distinct from the account owner in the sales organization.

## Sales records and decisions

**Opportunity**:
The account-level sales initiative that groups one or more Funnels.

**Funnel**:
A deal within an Opportunity that advances through pipeline stages.
_Avoid_: Opportunity when referring to one deal's stage

**Quotation**:
A versioned commercial proposal attached to a Funnel.
_Avoid_: Quote in user-facing labels

**Stage approval**:
A manager's decision on a request to advance a Funnel into a gated stage.

**Quotation approval**:
A manager's decision that authorizes a Quotation to be sent. It is separate from a customer's acceptance.

**Customer acceptance**:
The customer's agreement to a sent Quotation; the Funnel is marked Won through a separate stage change.
