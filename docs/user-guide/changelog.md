# Changelog & Release Notes

Stay updated with recent feature additions, quotation templates, and workflow improvements in Q-App.

---

## v1.2.155 — September 30, 2026

### 🎓 Quandatics Academy Quotation Template
* **New Template Layout**: Introduced the **Quandatics Academy** quotation template designed specifically for educational courses, workshops, certification training, and skill development programs.
* **Course Schedule & Delivery Blocks**: Includes formatted course outlines, trainer bios, participant capacity, and specialized training terms.

---

## v1.2.154 — September 30, 2026

### 🧾 Dynamic Quotation Tax Labels
* **Settings-Driven Tax Display**: Quotations now dynamically render tax labels and percentages directly from tenant tax settings (e.g., *SST 8%* vs. *Exempt*).
* **Tax Inclusive / Exclusive Precision**: Clarified tax calculation footers on client proposal PDFs to eliminate ambiguous rounding.

---

## v1.2.153 — September 29, 2026

### 👥 Pipeline Ownership Alignment
* **Automated Ownership Synchronization**: Imported opportunities and funnels automatically harmonize record ownership with the assigned parent Account owner.
* **Access Scope Consistency**: Prevents access discrepancies where sales representatives could view accounts but not their historical opportunities.

---

## v1.2.150 — September 25, 2026

### ⚡ Shared List Controls & Server-Side Pagination
* **High-Density Table Controls**: Redesigned list headers across Leads, Accounts, Opportunities, Funnel, Quotations, and Payment Milestones.
* **Server-Side Pagination**: Added fast, responsive pagination supporting **25**, **50**, or **100** records per page.
* **Private Saved Views**: Users can save custom filters, sorting rules, and column visibility preferences. Saved views are strictly private to each user account.

---

## v1.2.140 — August 28, 2026

### 💳 Payment Milestone Decoupling
* **Operational Cashflow Planning**: Streamlined payment milestones into clean commercial planning records (`Won` ➔ `Invoiced`).
* **Automated Won Trigger**: Closing an opportunity as **Closed Won** automatically transitions all active deal milestones to `Won`.
* **Safe Billing Transitions**: Enabled sales and finance teams to mark milestones as `Invoiced` without triggering premature ERP accounting side effects.

---

## v1.2.120 — August 15, 2026

### 🎯 PPVVC Qualification Methodology
* **Enterprise Sales Rigor**: Embedded the **PPVVC** framework (*Power Sponsor, Pain, Vision, Value, Control*) into the Opportunity container.
* **Automated Stage 4a Project Code**: Entering stage **`4a` (Commit)** now automatically reserves and allocates the official Delivery Project Code for pre-delivery planning.
